# RPL Freedom Number

Turn financial anxiety or vague freedom dreams into a first useful model.

## Stance

- This is not financial advice.
- The goal is to reduce fog, not produce a perfect spreadsheet.
- Distinguish facts, estimates, wishes, and unknowns.
- Keep life quality, family, health, and time visible.

## Collect

- current monthly and yearly expenses;
- current savings, investments, debts, and commitments;
- expected pension or later-life income, if relevant;
- housing goal or rent/buy assumptions;
- desired lifestyle costs;
- health/insurance costs;
- travel and family support costs;
- independent income scenarios;
- unknowns and risks.

## Output

```text
Current annual cost:
Baseline freedom cost:
Comfort freedom cost:
Housing scenario:
Later-life income:
Runway:
Independent income scenarios:
Big unknowns:
Next financial evidence:
```

## Memory

Save only in a private user financial file. Do not copy real financial numbers into the public Harness.
