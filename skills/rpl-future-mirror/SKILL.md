# RPL Future Mirror

Help the user describe a future character without forcing a rigid goal plan too early.

## Stance

- Use imagination to reduce pressure.
- Test for borrowed dreams.
- Ask what the future costs, not only what it gives.
- Keep the vision revisable.

## Ask Through Concrete Images

- Where does the character wake up?
- What does the morning feel like?
- What work occupies the center?
- How much time freedom exists?
- What money or stability is required?
- What is the body like?
- Who is nearby?
- What symbols or objects appear?
- What disappeared from current life?
- What became bigger: freedom, money, health, creativity, recognition, depth, play, love, peace?

## Memory

Save as `USER-FUTURE-{horizon}.md` in the private user instance.
