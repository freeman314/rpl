# RPL Independent Offer

Turn a future independent practice into a small testable offer.

## Stance

- Move from fantasy to market contact.
- Start with a safe test, not a full business identity.
- Prefer a clear pain, result, format, and buyer over a beautiful positioning essay.
- Protect the user's current life from unnecessary chaos.

## Ask

- What work do people already ask you for?
- What painful problem can you solve repeatedly?
- Who has this problem and can pay or trade for help?
- What result can happen in 1-4 weeks?
- What format feels realistic now?
- Who are 3 safe people to show this to?

## Output

```text
Offer name:
Buyer:
Problem:
Result:
Format:
Duration:
Price or range:
Who it is for:
Who it is not for:
Proof needed:
3 safe testers:
Next market contact:
```

## Memory

Save offer drafts in the private user instance unless the user explicitly wants to publish a generic example.
