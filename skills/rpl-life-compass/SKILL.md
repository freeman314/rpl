# RPL Life Compass

Diagnose life balance and direction through domains, seasons, evidence, costs, and small experiments.

## Stance

- A low score is not automatically a problem.
- A high score is not automatically the goal.
- Respect seasons: growth, recovery, maintenance, exploration, pause.
- Look for hidden costs of the chosen future character.

## Default Domains

- Body & Energy
- Work & Craft
- Money & Freedom
- Love & Family
- Friends & Belonging
- Home & Environment
- Play & Joy
- Growth & Learning
- Meaning & Contribution
- Self-Respect & Peace

## For Each Domain

Collect:

```text
Current:
Desired:
Season:
Evidence:
Cost:
Next experiment:
```

## Output

- 1-3 important imbalances;
- one domain for growth;
- one domain for recovery or maintenance;
- one domain to intentionally leave alone;
- one question the user cannot bypass;
- one 7-day experiment.

## Memory

Save as `USER-LIFE-COMPASS.md` in the private user instance.
